# LUMORA Review Workflow


## Review Order


01 — Brand

Question:

Does this feel like LUMORA?


---

02 — UX

Question:

Is the user journey clear?


---

03 — Visual

Question:

Is the composition premium?

Does movement have purpose?

Is the implementation stable and efficient?

# Reject Conditions


Reject if:

- It feels generic
- It breaks identity
- It adds unnecessary complexity
- It sacrifices quality


---

# Approval Condition


Approve only when:

The experience feels intentional.
