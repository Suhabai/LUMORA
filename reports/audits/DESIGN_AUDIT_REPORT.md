# Findings

## Finding #001

Severity:

`Recommendation`

Location:

DESIGN.md — Design System Governance

Issue:

The design system defines visual identity and design principles effectively, but it does not explicitly define implementation boundaries.

Impact:

As the LUMORA ecosystem expands, future contributors may unintentionally introduce visual patterns that conflict with the established design language.

Required Action:

Optional.

Consider adding an "Implementation Boundaries" section that clearly distinguishes approved design patterns from discouraged ones.

---

## Finding #002

Severity:

`Recommendation`

Location:

DESIGN.md — Future Design Evolution

Issue:

The document establishes a strong foundation but does not describe how future design decisions should be evaluated before becoming part of the official design system.

Impact:

Without a defined evolution process, design consistency may gradually decrease as new projects are added.

Required Action:

Optional.

Consider defining a Design Evolution Policy that specifies how new visual patterns, components, or interaction styles are proposed, reviewed, and approved.
