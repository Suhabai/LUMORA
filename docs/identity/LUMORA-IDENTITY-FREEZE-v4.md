# LUMORA Identity Freeze v4

## Status

**Identity Foundation:** FROZEN

**Current production identity authority:** This document supersedes
`LUMORA-IDENTITY-FREEZE-v3.md`. v3 remains historical documentation only.

**Primary identity:** LUMORA Luminous Ring

## 1. Primary Mark

The approved primary mark is the luminous LUMORA ring: a near-circular,
liquid-light identity mark with a transparent center, organic contour,
irregular interruptions, naturally varying apparent thickness, concentrated
white-hot luminous regions, violet body light, controlled outer bloom, and
asymmetric luminous droplets/beads with non-uniform size and spacing.

The mark is premium, cinematic, calm, distinctive, and completely still.

### Canonical production assets

- `public/brand/lumora-mark-luminous.png`
- `public/brand/lumora-mark-luminous.webp`
- `public/brand/lumora-mark-flat.png`

### Approved visual reference

`experiments/identity-v4-logo/references/final-approved-lumora-logo.png`

The approved raster luminous asset is the visual source of truth. Do not
redraw it from memory, reconstruct it as a simplified SVG, normalize its
irregularities, evenly space droplets, remove interruptions, alter droplet
positions, or replace it with earlier Eclipse, M Core, or Candidate Alpha
marks. A future vector reconstruction requires a separately opened identity
review phase and explicit human approval.

## 2. Wordmark

The production wordmark is real text:

`LUMORA`

It uses the existing project sans-serif typography system with:

- uppercase letters
- font weight `300`
- letter-spacing `0.32em`
- clean geometric sans-serif appearance
- quiet hierarchy secondary to the luminous symbol
- no custom Candidate Alpha O or R
- no experimental glyph overlaps
- no glow
- no animation

The production relationship is:

`[Luminous Ring]  L U M O R A`

The wordmark is not baked into the luminous raster asset for normal UI use.

## 4. Background and Flat Behavior

The luminous primary mark has a transparent center. On dark environments the
center naturally reads dark; on light environments the background shows
through. Do not add a permanent opaque black center without a controlled
identity review.

`public/brand/lumora-mark-flat.png` is the approved flat/monochrome fallback
for monochrome contexts, constrained presentation, and cases where luminous
treatment is unsuitable. It is not the primary expressive identity.

## 5. Still Identity Rule

The identity itself remains still. Do not add pulse, breathing, orbit,
rotation, morphing, reactive glow, animated droplets, particle emission, or
loading-spinner rotation. A future Signature Loading Experience may draw on
the brand's light language, but must not silently animate the frozen identity
mark. Any identity animation requires a separately documented review.

## 6. Living Core Separation

**Luminous Ring = brand identity.**

**Living Core = experiential environmental system.**

They are architecturally distinct. Do not transfer Living Core PRESENCE,
heartbeat, morphing, state, interaction logic, glow, or animation to the
brand identity.

## 7. Sonic Rule

LUMORA remains intentionally silent under the canonical Sonic Grammar. The
identity does not add a sonic logo, logo sound, heartbeat sound, loading sound,
or ambience.

## 8. Color and Effect Rule

The approved violet and white-light treatment is identity behavior. Do not
introduce new brand colors, arbitrary recolors, rainbow variants, generic
neon effects on the wordmark, or excessive persistent glow. Navigation and
footer identity must remain restrained and quiet.

## 9. Deprecated Legacy Identity

The following are no longer current production identity:

- Candidate Alpha / Quiet C wordmark geometry
- old M Core as the primary logo
- Eclipse Core A1/A2/A3 experiments
- black-hole refinement experiments
- reference-locked ring experiments
- old custom Candidate Alpha wordmark SVG
- `app/icon.svg`

They are historical only where retained in Git history.

## 10. Change Control

Future phases must not alter the Luminous Ring geometry, raster source of
truth, wordmark character, lockup relationship, transparent-center behavior,
stillness rule, color behavior, or Living Core separation without an
explicitly opened identity review phase and human approval.

Motion Grammar remains authoritative for all motion. Sonic Grammar remains
authoritative for the intentional NO SOUND decision.
