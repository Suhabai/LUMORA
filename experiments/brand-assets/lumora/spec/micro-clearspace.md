# LUMORA — Micro-Mark Clear Space

Phase 0C.20

---

## Principle

Clear space prevents the V-dip from visually merging with surrounding elements.

---

## Measurement Basis

~0.125x mark width as minimum optical breathing room.

| Size | Mark Width | Clear Space |
|------|-----------|-------------|
| 64px | 64px | ~8px each side |
| 32px | 32px | ~4px each side |
| 24px | 24px | ~3px each side |
| 16px | 16px | ~2px each side |

---

## What Clear Space Protects

- The V-dip notch remains visually distinct
- The two vertical stems do not merge with adjacent elements
- The mark maintains its structural identity at micro scale

---

## Favicon Context

At favicon size (16px), the clear space is approximately 2px. This is sufficient because the favicon square itself provides containment.

---

## Prohibited

Do not place elements inside the clear space. Do not crowd the micro-mark against navigation text or other UI elements.
