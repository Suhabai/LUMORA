# LUMORA — Wordmark Clear Space

Phase 0E.2.4 — Candidate Alpha (Quiet C)

---

## Principle

Clear space is based on optical breathing room around the wordmark geometry. Not arbitrary mathematical rules.

---

## Measurement Basis

The clear space is derived from half the stroke width (35u) as minimum breathing room around the wordmark.

| Direction | Minimum | Derived From |
|-----------|---------|--------------|
| Left | ~40u | ~0.057 x cap height |
| Right | ~40u | ~0.057 x cap height |
| Top | ~35u | ~0.05 x cap height |
| Bottom | ~35u | ~0.05 x cap height |

---

## Optical Sizes

### Large (120px+)

Full clear space. The wordmark has room to breathe at display sizes.

### Medium (48-119px)

Proportionally maintained. Clear space scales with the wordmark.

### Small (24-47px)

Reduced proportionally. The wordmark relies more on letter rhythm than surrounding space.

---

## Practical Minimum

The wordmark should not be used below 24px cap height. Below this size, a separate secondary mark (M Core) is required.

---

## What Clear Space Protects

- The O counter (rx=227) remains visually distinct
- The M junction (y=370) maintains its proportional depth
- The wordmark does not merge with surrounding elements
- The letter spacing rhythm is preserved

---

## Candidate Alpha Differences from Direction C

Candidate Alpha's refined optical corrections (O rx=227 vs 225, M y=370 vs 375) create slightly more balanced internal spacing. The clear space values remain proportional because they derive from cap height, not internal letter dimensions.

No clear space adjustment is required.

---

## Prohibited

Do not place elements inside the clear space. Do not crowd the wordmark against other content.
