H. WORDMARK MINIMUM-SIZE ISSUES

  • Below 16px height: Letterforms begin to merge. M diagonal loses definition.
  • 16–18px height: Small variant required. Acceptable for nav but not ideal.
  • 20–24px height: Sweet spot for navigation. Small variant fully legible.
  • 24–28px height: Medium variant. Full detail preserved. Ideal for desktop.
  • Key issue: Wordmark alone at mobile sizes (16–18px) loses the distinctive M/O/R character that defines the brand. The optical simplifications in the small variant help, but the wordmark's identity advantage diminishes at this scale.

I. MICRO-MARK MINIMUM-SIZE ISSUES

  • 16px (M-16 variant): Undercut removed, purest V-dip. Works perfectly at 16px.
  • 20–24px (M-Optical): Undercut preserved, diagonal simplified. Clear at all sizes.
  • 28px+ (M-Master): Full structural detail. All features visible.
  • No minimum-size issues. M Core is designed specifically for this scale range. The three-tier variant system (master → optical → 16) ensures clean rendering at any size.
