Specification Rationale
This lockup was derived through systematic testing of 64+ combinations across desktop, tablet, mobile, and footer contexts. The chosen values maintain identity coherence while respecting the practical constraints of each viewport.
Key decisions:
• Desktop and tablet share the same lockup (133px) — no unnecessary reduction when space exists.
• Mobile drops to M Core only — the wordmark cannot maintain optical corrections below 24px height.
• Footer uses same lockup as nav — brand reinforcement at page exit, space available.
• 8px gap is the consistent connector — not too tight, not too loose.
• Vertical center alignment — simplest, most reliable across browsers and renders.
