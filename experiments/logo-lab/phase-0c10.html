Overview — Controlled Compounding

Phase 0C.9 identified three modifications with meaningful perceptual impact:
MO-A (junction undercut), MO-B (counter relationship), OR-C (R-leg release).
This phase tests whether they compound coherently, interfere, or become decorative.

Control Conditions
BASE

No modifications. Neutral geometric sans-serif control.

TEST 01 — STRUCTURAL

MO-A + OR-C. Junction undercut + R-leg release. No counter change.

TEST 02 — COMPOUND

MO-A + MO-B + OR-C. All three modifications active.

TEST 03 — COUNTER/RELEASE

MO-B + OR-C. Counter + release. No junction undercut.

What Is Modified
MO-A (Junction) — M right stem undercut: bottom extends -15u inward, -8u horizontal. Creates micro-gap at M→O boundary.
MO-B (Counter) — M V-dip deepened: 280u→340u from cap top. O counter widened: inner radius scaled 1.4→1.2.
OR-C (Release) — R leg endpoint shifted +200u right. Bowl counter adjusted. Leg angle widened.
What Is NOT Modified
L/U — unmodified
U/M — unmodified
R/A — unmodified
L letterform — unmodified
U letterform — unmodified
A letterform — unmodified
