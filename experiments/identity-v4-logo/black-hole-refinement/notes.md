# LUMORA Black-Hole Identity Refinement

## Scope

BH1–BH3 are controlled, static refinements of A2 Balanced Eclipse. They explore swallowed light, gravitational pull, and dark-core tension without reopening the broader identity system.

## BH1 — Quiet Lensing

The seam is smallest and the circular reading is most complete. This is the calmest study, with the lowest risk of spectacle and the highest risk of reading as a conventional crescent at tiny sizes.

## BH2 — Balanced Accretion

The seam has moderate directional tension and the strongest balance between ring continuity and a dark-core reading. It is the neutral benchmark for judging cinematic presence against premium restraint.

## BH3 — Deep Eclipse

The deeper interruption and heavier ring create the clearest sense of swallowed light and gravitational pull. The tradeoff is greater risk of generic ring or portal readings if the seam becomes too dominant.

## Shared constraints

- All marks are still and non-reactive.
- No pulse, orbit, morph, breathing, or animated glow.
- No sound; Sonic Grammar and the NO SOUND decision remain unchanged.
- No Living Core behavior is transferred to the identity.
- All candidates are shown with and without glow, in monochrome, as lockups, and at 16px / 24px / 32px / 48px.
- The LUMORA wordmark is presented consistently and remains secondary to the symbol.

## Review prompts

Evaluate light-swallowing character, small-size recognition, monochrome strength, glow independence, originality, favicon potential, and calm premium restraint. These studies do not select a winner or authorize production integration.
