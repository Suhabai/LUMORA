# Black-Hole Visual Review

This review presents the existing BH1, BH2, and BH3 directions without changing their geometry.

Review each direction for:

- cinematic swallowed-light character
- stillness and premium restraint
- seam authorship
- 16px / 24px / 32px / 48px recognition
- monochrome and no-glow independence
- wordmark balance
- favicon-scale potential
- risk of reading as a generic crescent, ring, portal, reactor, or spinner

The review does not authorize production integration or select a final direction. The current production identity remains canonical.
