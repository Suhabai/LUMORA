# LUMORA Final Approved Mark v2 — Reconstruction Notes

## Source

The approved PNG reference was inspected directly. V2 is a new reconstruction, not a continuation of the earlier crescent geometry and not an overwrite of the v1 draft.

## Layer model

- Base geometry: four custom Bézier segments form an organic near-complete ring around a transparent center.
- Violet body: deep-to-bright violet body light with locally varied segment weights.
- White-hot highlight: concentrated upper-right light sector and smaller lower-right inner glint.
- Inner light: restrained bright edge accents.
- Outer bloom: static controlled blur only.
- Droplet light: six unequal beads distributed across different regions and distances.

## Fidelity intent

The mark is deliberately less mechanically perfect than a circle while remaining immediately circular. Breaks are unequal, thickness varies by segment, and beads are not evenly spaced. `lumora-mark-flat.svg` preserves the same organic silhouette and bead geometry without glow.

## Review limitation

The supplied PNG is a presentation board rather than an isolated vector source. V2 therefore targets its visible character—liquid-light construction, hot highlight, organic interruptions, asymmetric droplets, and transparent center—without claiming pixel-identical tracing.
