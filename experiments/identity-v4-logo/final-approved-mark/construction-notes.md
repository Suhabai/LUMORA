# LUMORA Final Approved Mark — Construction Notes

## Reference

The approved reference was inspected before construction. It establishes a luminous near-complete ring, deep transparent void, violet/white-hot light, organic interruptions, and naturally asymmetric bead elements.

## Geometry

- The center is transparent; no black fill is encoded.
- The ring is composed of four near-complete arc segments with small, unequal interruptions.
- Beads are separate asymmetric circles with varied sizes and positions.
- No animation, pulse, orbit, morph, breathing, particles, or sound are present.

## Two layers

`lumora-mark-flat.svg` is the clean scalable silhouette using `currentColor`. `lumora-mark.svg` uses the same arc and bead geometry with restrained violet/white luminance and a static bloom filter for presentation. `lumora-lockup.svg` pairs the luminous mark with the existing quiet tracked wordmark treatment.

## Verification intent

The application board tests dark and light backgrounds, transparent center behavior, luminous and no-glow presentations, monochrome behavior, 16px / 24px / 32px / 48px / 64px / 128px sizes, horizontal lockup, compact header, and favicon-style surfaces.
