# LUMORA Luminous Black-Hole Identity

## Scope

L1–L3 are controlled experiments derived from the black-hole direction. The intent is a light-formed identity mark: a dense luminous structure gathered around a dark central void.

## L1 — Quiet Accretion

The most restrained concentration. The outer ring remains calm and legible while a short inner arc suggests light gathering without spectacle.

## L2 — Balanced Singularity

The neutral benchmark. The void, outer ring, seam, and inner concentration share the clearest balance between luminous tension and premium restraint.

## L3 — Deep Event Horizon

The most dramatic study. Its larger elliptical contour and stronger inner arc create the clearest swallowed-light reading, with increased risk of generic ring or sci-fi associations.
