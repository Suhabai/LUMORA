Phase 0D.2 — Research Lab

Sonic Grammar

Evaluation Per-Behavior

Architectural Decision

Final Position

No Sound

LUMORA is silent by default.

Sound adds no information, no orientation, and no meaningful emotional depth that is not already communicated through the visual system. The experience is complete without any auditory layer.

Evaluation Framework

8 Questions

Every potential use of sound was evaluated against eight questions. Sound failed to meet the threshold for inclusion on every criterion.

A

Does sound add information?

All information is communicated through typography, layout, color, hierarchy, and motion.

No
B

Does sound add orientation?

Spatial orientation is handled by the motion grammar (HANDOFF, ALIGN, REVEAL).

No
C

Does sound improve interaction feedback?

Visual micro-interaction feedback already exists. Sound would be redundant.

No
D

Does sound increase emotional depth?

Emotional sound easily becomes cinematic decoration — the exact anti-pattern LUMORA avoids.

No
E

Does sound improve continuity?

Continuity is handled by HANDOFF motion. Sound would compete, not improve.

No
F

Does sound create cognitive load?

Any sound requires processing. Silence is cognitive rest. Sound is cognitive occupation.

Yes — harmful
G

Does sound conflict with calm?

LUMORA's calm is experiential. Any sound breaks silence and shifts the experience.

Yes — harmful
H

Can the same meaning be communicated visually?

Every potential sonic meaning is already communicated through the six motion vocabulary behaviors.

Yes — redundant

Reopening Conditions

Future Consideration

This decision may be revisited only if all five conditions are met:

  1. 01 A specific, documented user need emerges that sound uniquely satisfies
  2. 02 The need cannot be met through visual means
  3. 03 The sound does not conflict with calm, focus, or premium character
  4. 04 The sound is semantically justified, not decorative
  5. 05 A new identity review phase is explicitly opened
