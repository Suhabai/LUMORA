Evaluation Per-Behavior

Motion Behavior × Sound

6 Behaviors Evaluated

Each of the six motion vocabulary behaviors was evaluated for sonic potential. All six are intentionally silent.

R

REVEAL

Semantic meaning: "This exists and is now visible." Visual reveal (opacity, translation, clip-path) is sufficient. Sound would either redundantly announce what the eye already sees, or create auditory attention that competes with visual discovery.

Silent
A

ALIGN

Semantic meaning: "These elements belong together." Visual settling (staggered entrance, spatial composition) is sufficient. Sound cannot communicate spatial relationship as precisely as visual composition.

Silent
R

RELEASE

Semantic meaning: "The constraint is lifted." Visual release (clip-path opening, scale expansion) is sufficient. A "release" sound would be cinematic decoration — exactly the anti-pattern LUMORA avoids.

Silent
D

DRIFT

Semantic meaning: "The environment is alive." Atmospheric visual movement is sufficient. Adding atmospheric sound would create a soundscape. LUMORA is not a soundscape.

Silent
H

HANDOFF

Semantic meaning: "The world continues beyond what you see." Spatial continuity is handled by visual spatial mapping. A "transition" sound would be a cinematic transition effect — the exact anti-pattern the motion grammar forbids.

Silent
P

PRESENCE

Semantic meaning: "This system is conscious." The Living Core's visual breathing is sufficient. Adding a "heartbeat" sound would merge the sensory grammars and create a multi-sensory logo — dangerously close to the identity anti-pattern.

Silent

Hypothetical Sonic Vocabulary

Evaluated and Rejected

If sound were justified, five concepts were investigated. Every one maps directly to an existing visual behavior.

Arrival Something enters the experience Redundant with REVEAL
Connection Elements establish relationship Redundant with ALIGN
Release Constraint lifts Redundant with RELEASE
Continuity Spatial handoff Redundant with HANDOFF
Atmosphere Environmental presence Would create soundscape

Sonic Anti-Patterns

Explicitly Forbidden

Universal

  • Constant background music
  • Autoplay ambience
  • Every-click sound
  • Fake futuristic beeps
  • Heavy bass impacts
  • Cinematic whoosh spam
  • Notification-style repetition
  • Excessive stereo movement
  • Audio that competes with text

LUMORA-Specific

  • Living Core "heartbeat" — merges sensory grammars
  • Logo sound — identity remains visual only
  • Sonic reveal — redundant with visual REVEAL
  • Sonic alignment — cannot communicate spatial relationship
  • Sonic release — cinematic decoration
  • Sonic atmosphere — creates soundscape
  • Sonic handoff — cinematic transition effect
  • Sound-only information — violates accessibility
  • Sound-dependent navigation — non-functional without audio
  • Sound to hide weak visual feedback
