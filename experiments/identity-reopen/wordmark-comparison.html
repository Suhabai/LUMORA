M Core Compatibility

Direction A: M Core proven compatible in production. Stems at 258/328/664/734 fit within M letterform (1130-1602). No conflict.

Direction B: M stems tightened to 1130-1595. M Core is wider than the condensed M. At 24px+, the Core may appear broader than the wordmark M. Requires visual testing.
