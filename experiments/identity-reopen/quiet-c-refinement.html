Section 5 — Selected Candidates (Top 3)

Candidate Alpha — M-370 × R-B × O-227

What makes it quieter: The M junction rises 5 units (shallower V), reducing dramatic diagonal tension. The R bowl contracts 15 units and the leg pulls 28 units inward, softening the kick. The O tightens 2 units on outer/counter. The cumulative effect is a wordmark that feels more composed — authorship is present but restrained. At small scale, readability is fully maintained.

Candidate Beta — M-375 × R-B × O-227

What makes it quieter: Keeps the proven M junction at y=375 — the M's character is unchanged. The R bowl and leg are tightened identically to Candidate Alpha. The O is subtly refined. This is the conservative path: only one letter (R) is meaningfully adjusted. Lowest risk of identity loss.

Candidate Gamma — M-370 × R-C × O-227

What makes it quieter: The most restrained combination tested. The M junction is shallower (y=370), the R is fully neutralized (bowl -35u, leg -58u). The R approaches standard proportions — the authored character is minimized. The O is refined. Risk: at small scale, the R may lose its distinctive identity. Best suited for contexts where the wordmark is not the primary focus.
